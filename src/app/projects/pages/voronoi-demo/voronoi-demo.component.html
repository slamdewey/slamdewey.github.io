Voronoi

A Voronoi diagram splits a surface into cells, one per seed: every point joins the seed it sits closest to. This is an interactive playground for three surfaces — a flat plane, a cylinder, and a sphere.

Playground

How it works

The plane and cylinder use d3-delaunay; the sphere uses d3-geo-voronoi. A small adapter, voronoi-edges.ts, sits on top and gives every surface the same shape of output:

  • One set of types for cells and edges, shared across all three surfaces.
  • Cylinder wrapping, by copying each seed one width left and right before tessellating.
  • Neighbors found by matching shared polygon edges, so every edge is a real, visible boundary.
  • Lloyd relaxation that nudges each seed toward the center of its cell to even things out.
