Voronoi Tessellation

1. Seeds

Deterministic xorshift32 PRNG drops N points on the plane.

2. Cells & Lloyd Relaxation

Every pixel claims its nearest seed (raw). Each iteration nudges seeds toward their cell's centroid, evening out the sizes.

3. Cylindrical Wrapping

For world maps, the x-axis must seam — distance and centroid math both go modulo width.

4. Adjacency Graph

One pass over the pixel grid yields the dual graph — who's neighbors with whom — for downstream simulation.

5. Spherical Wrapping

Same idea, two dimensions of wrap: cells live on a sphere. The same data shows as an orthographic globe and as a mercator projection — which itself wraps forever along x, like every world map ever.

Drag to rotate the globe.
