Voronoi Tessellation

A Voronoi diagram partitions a surface into cells, with every point belonging to its nearest seed. This page is an interactive playground for three topologies — non-wrapping, cylindrical, and spherical — backed by a small adapter that exposes a unified vector-edge data structure across all three.

Adapter overview

Planar and cylindrical tessellations are computed with d3-delaunay; the sphere uses d3-geo-voronoi. The voronoi-edges.ts adapter wraps both libraries and provides:

  • Unified types for cells, boundary edges, and shared-edge endpoints across all three topologies.
  • Cylindrical-wrap support via ghost-copy preprocessing: each seed is replicated at x ± width and planar Delaunay runs on the extended point set.
  • Adjacency by polygon-edge matching: two cells are reported as neighbors when their clipped polygons share a boundary segment inside the canvas, so edge endpoints are always real polygon vertices.
  • Lloyd relaxation driven by the analytical tessellation, moving each seed toward its cell's centroid on every iteration.
