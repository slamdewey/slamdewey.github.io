Voronoi Tessellation

1. Seeds

Deterministic xorshift32 PRNG drops N points on the plane.

2. Cells & Lloyd Relaxation

Every pixel claims its nearest seed (raw). Each iteration nudges seeds toward their cell's centroid, evening out the sizes.

3. Cylindrical Wrapping

For world-map strips, the x-axis must seam. Distance and the centroid pass both go modulo width — angular averaging in sin/cos space keeps the centroid pass from breaking at the wrap.

No wrapping — hard seams at left/right edges.

Wrapping on — drag to pan across the seam.

4. Spherical Wrapping

Cells live on a unit sphere. Seeds are unit vectors; the nearest-seed loop becomes a max-dot-product over those vectors. The same data drives an orthographic globe and a mercator projection — drag either, both stay in sync.

Drag to rotate the globe.

Mercator — drag to pan; the globe rotates with it.

5. Adjacency Graph

One pass over the pixel grid yields the dual graph — who's neighbors with whom — for downstream simulation.
